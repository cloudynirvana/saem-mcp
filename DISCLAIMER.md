# Disclaimer

SAEM MCP is a **research simulation toolkit**. It exposes in-silico cancer-dynamics tools over the [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) (MCP).

It is **not**:

- a medical device
- a clinical decision support (CDS) system
- a clinically validated diagnostic, prognostic, or therapeutic product
- dosing, regimen, or treatment advice for any patient

Outputs such as “cure rate,” “protocol,” “validation gates,” and resistance comparisons are **computational / in-silico scores** from a simulation engine. They are not clinical endpoints, not trial results, and not a claim of Phase I–III evidence.

Do not use these tools to choose, justify, or withhold care. Any translational use requires independent preclinical work, clinical studies, and regulatory review outside this repository.
